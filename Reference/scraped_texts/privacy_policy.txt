Privacy Policy
We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting your personal information. We only collect essential details needed to process orders, enhance our services, and deliver an improved customer experience.
1.  Information We Collect
We collect basic personal details such as name, email address, phone number, and shipping information when an order is placed or a inquiry is made. This helps us process requests efficiently and improve the customer experience.
2. How We Use Your Information
Personal information is used to process orders, respond to inquiries, and enhance our services. It also helps us ensure a smooth and personalized user experience.
3. Data Protection
We apply strong security measures to safeguard personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. All information is stored securely and treated with strict confidentiality.
4. Sharing of Information
Personal data is not sold, traded, or rented to third parties. It is only shared when necessary to fulfill orders, such as with shipping or payment service providers.
5. Cookies
Cookies may be used to improve website performance and enhance user experience. Users can disable cookies anytime through their browser settings if preferred.
6. Your Rights
Users have the right to access, update, or request deletion of their personal data at any time. Support is available for any data-related concerns.
7. Contact Us
For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, users can reach out through the available support or contact to our social media pages.
