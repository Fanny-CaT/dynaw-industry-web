Faqs
Help & Faqs
Find answers to the most frequently asked questions about our products, services, customization options, ordering process, and policies. We provide clear and helpful information to ensure a smooth experience and support you with any queries you may have.
What customization options do you offer for products?
We offer screen printing, embroidery, heat transfer, sublimation, and private labeling to meet different design and branding needs.
What is the minimum order quantity for production?
Our minimum order quantity varies depending on the product type and customization requirements. Please contact us for specific details.
How long does the manufacturing and delivery process take?
Production and delivery time depends on order size and design complexity, but we always aim to complete orders within the agreed timeline.
Do you provide samples before bulk production?
Yes, we provide samples or prototypes to ensure design accuracy and customer approval before starting bulk production.
What payment methods and policies do you accept?
We accept multiple secure payment methods and follow clear policies to ensure a smooth and transparent ordering process.
Can I request custom designs for my order?
Yes, we offer full customization services where you can share your design ideas, and our team will develop them according to your requirements.
What types of fabrics do you use?
We use high-quality, durable, and performance-based fabrics, including certified and sustainable options, based on customer needs.
Do you offer bulk order discounts?
Yes, we provide competitive pricing and special discounts for bulk and long-term orders.
How do you ensure product quality?
We follow strict quality control procedures, including material testing, in-process monitoring, and final inspection before delivery.
Can I track my order during production or shipping?
Yes, we provide updates during production and tracking details once the order is shipped for full transparency.
